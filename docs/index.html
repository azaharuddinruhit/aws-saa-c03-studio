How this is estimated
  • A right answer to a hard question counts for more than one to an easy question, and lucky guesses are allowed for (25% on four options, 10% on a Select TWO).
  • Your level is projected onto the real exam's mix of easy, medium and hard questions, so practising easy ones won't inflate it.
  • Domains are weighted by the blueprint, and a domain with few answers leans on your overall level.
  • Recent answers count more (21-day half-life). All retries of a question together count at most 40% of a fresh answer, and less when retried soon after. An answer after reading the hint counts as an easier question.
  • 3,000 simulated 50-question exams give the 80% range and the chance of reaching 720. The range widens the longer you go without practising.
  • A refresh never changes it, but small moves are within its margin of error. AWS doesn't publish how answers convert to its scale, so treat it as a guide.
